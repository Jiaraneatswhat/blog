<p style="margin:0!important;text-indent:0!important;font-family: 'Gulliver' !important;"><span style="background:#000000;color:#ffffff;font-weight:bold;padding:0 4px;user-select:none;">ANIMAL BEHAVIOR</span></p>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">**THE EUROPEAN BLISTER BEETLE** lays thousands of eggs in the spring. When they hatch, the bright-orange larvae <mark style="background:#fdbfff">shimmy</mark>[^1] up flower stems and sit in clumps, waiting for passing solitary bees to latch on to with hooklike appendages so they can grab a lift. Now researchers have found that these clumps give off a distinctly floral scent—making the larvae the first animal known to mimic a flower’s smell.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">欧洲斑蝥会在春季产下数千枚卵。虫卵孵化后，通体亮橙色的幼虫沿着花茎蜿蜒爬到高处，成群聚集，等候路过的独居蜜蜂靠近；它们借助钩状附肢攀附在蜜蜂身上，搭便车前往蜂巢。如今研究人员发现，这些幼虫集群会散发出浓郁的花香，这也让它们成为已知首例能够模拟花香气味的动物。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Ryan Alam, a chemist at the Max Planck Institute for Chemical Ecology in Germany, and his colleagues found that the larvae attract bees by emitting a collection of 17 scented compounds often found in flowers, including <mark style="background:rgba(136, 49, 204, 0.2)">linalool</mark>[^2] oxide and lilac aldehyde. Once a larva has been airlifted to a bee’s nest, it feeds on the bees’eggs and supplies of pollen and nectar. It stays there until it <mark style="background:#fdbfff">pupates</mark>[^3] and then leaves as an adult to restart the cycle.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">德国马克斯・普朗克化学生态学研究所化学家瑞安・阿拉姆及其团队发现，这类幼虫会释放17 种常见于花卉中的香气化合物来吸引蜜蜂，其中包括氧化芳樟醇与紫丁香醛。一旦幼虫被蜜蜂空运带到蜂巢，便以蜂卵、花粉和花蜜为食。它会一直留在巢中直至化蛹，之后羽化为成虫离开，开启新一轮生命周期。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">In addition to attracting bees, the larvae’s perfume also draws other larvae, which could help them to form those flowerlike aggregations. The study was posted on the preprint server bioRxiv and hasn’t been peer-reviewed, but it “presents a convincing case that the beetle larvae are mimicking flowers chemically, and perhaps visually, so as to deceive and attract bees,” says Jim McLean, an evolutionary biologist at Macquarie University in Australia, who was not involved in the study.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">除了吸引蜜蜂，幼虫释放的香气还能吸引其他同类幼虫，有助于它们聚集成形似花丛的群体。该研究已发布在预印本平台 bioRxiv 上，尚未经过同行评审。但澳大利亚麦考瑞大学演化生物学家吉姆・麦克林（并未参与此项研究）表示：“这项研究给出了极具说服力的证据，证明这类斑蝥幼虫在化学层面模拟花香，或许还存在视觉拟态，以此欺骗并吸引蜜蜂。”</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Animals such as the orchid mantis mimic the look of flowers, and the corpse flower emits a smell like rotting meat to attract insects, but these beetles add a new strategy to the mimicry playbook, Alam says.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">阿拉姆称，兰花螳螂这类动物会模拟花朵外形，腐尸花则散发腐肉气味以引诱昆虫，而这种斑蝥为生物拟态的生存策略增添了全新类型。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Dmitry Telnov, an <mark style="background:#fdbfff">entomologist</mark>[^4] at the Natural History Museum in London, who was also not involved in the study, notes that a different blister beetle species in the U.S. can mimic the sex pheromones of host bees. Mimicking smells “might be an evolutionary approach used by blister beetles to attract specific species,” he says, “which is absolutely surprising.”</span>

<font color="#548dd4">伦敦自然历史博物馆昆虫学家德米特里・特尔诺夫同样未参与这项研究。他指出，美国另一种斑蝥能够模拟宿主蜜蜂的性信息素。他表示，模拟气味 “或许是斑蝥为吸引特定物种而演化出的生存策略，这着实令人惊叹。”</font>

<p style="font-family: 'Gulliver' !important;" align="right">—<i>Chris Simms</i></p>
