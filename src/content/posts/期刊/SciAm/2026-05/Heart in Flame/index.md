<p style="margin:0!important;text-indent:0!important;font-family: 'Gulliver' !important;"><span style="background:#000000;color:#ffffff;font-weight:bold;padding:0 4px;user-select:none;">HEALTH</span></p>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">DOCTORS HAVE BEEN DRILLED FOR decades on the four big risks for heart disease, which kills more Americans every year than any other illness. The fearsome foursome: hypertension, smoking, high levels of “bad” LDL cholesterol and type 2 diabetes. Yet for just as long cardiologists have seen patients who have none of these problems die from heart <mark style="background:#fdbfff">ailments</mark>[^1]. And the heart specialists haven’t had the slightest idea why.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">数十年来，医生们反复熟记引发心脏病的四大高危因素。心脏病每年致死人数高居美国各类疾病之首。这四大致命诱因分别是：高血压、吸烟、低密度脂蛋白（坏胆固醇）偏高以及</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{2}$ <font color="#548dd4">型糖尿病。但长久以来，心脏病专科医生总能遇到一类患者：他们不存在上述任何问题，却依旧死于心脏疾病，而医学界对此始终百思不得其解。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Up to a quarter of the people admitted to hospitals for heart attacks don’t have any of these four risk factors. Mysteriously, these “low-risk” heart disease patients actually have the worst outcomes. A 2023 analysis found that hospitalized acute coronary patients without any of the four hazards were 57 percent more likely to die compared with those who had at least one.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">因心脏病发作入院的患者中，多达四分之一并不具备上述四项风险因素。蹊跷的是，这类 “低风险” 心脏病患者预后情况反而最差。</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{2023}$ <font color="#548dd4">年一项研究分析显示，无任一四项致病高危因素的急性冠心病住院患者，死亡概率比至少存在一项危险因素的患者高出</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{57\%}$<font color="#548dd4">。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">If the big known risk factors miss one in four patients, they still predict trouble as expected for the remaining three. That’s a good record. But it also means that of the roughly 920,000 Americans who die of cardiovascular disease every year, about 230,000 of them will have done so for no understandable reason.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">虽说这几类已知主要高危因素，无法筛查出四分之一的患病群体，但对其余四分之三人群，依旧能精准预判发病风险，这套筛查体系整体成效尚可。可这也意味着，美国每年约</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{920,000}$ <font color="#548dd4">死于心血管疾病的人群里，有约</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{230,000}$ <font color="#548dd4">人的致死原因至今无从解释。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">This deadly puzzle has haunted cardiologist Paul Ridker for years. “I remember saying to myself that there must be some other fundamental determinant of heart disease,” says Ridker, who is director of the Center for Cardiovascular Disease Prevention at Brigham and Women’s Hospital in Boston.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">这个致命难题多年来一直困扰着心脏病专家保罗・里德克。任职于波士顿布莱根妇女医院心血管疾病预防中心主任的里德克表示：“我时常暗自思索，心脏病必然还存在其他根本性致病因素。”</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Dozens of studies and clinical trials later, Ridker thinks he has found the missing piece. His work, along with that of other researchers, now suggests that chronic <mark style="background:#fdbfff">inflammation</mark>[^2]—a prolonged and body-damaging state of immune system activation—may be the hidden factor that accelerates cardiovascular problems to a dangerous and deadly state. When cholesterol builds up in the arteries, it shape-shifts into a sharp and jagged form the body no longer recognizes, provoking the immune system to wage war against it and blood vessels. It is a battle with no winners, and the wreckage it leaves behind ends in heart attacks and strokes.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">历经数十项研究与临床试验后，里德克认为自己找到了这一缺失的关键诱因。他与其他学者的研究均表明：慢性炎症——即免疫系统长期处于亢奋状态、持续损伤身体的一种状态，或是暗藏的元凶，会加速心血管病变，直至发展成凶险致命的病症。胆固醇在动脉血管内堆积后，会发生形态异变，变成机体无法识别的尖锐粗糙物质，进而触发免疫系统对其以及血管本身发起攻击。这场拉锯战没有赢家，最终留下的身体损伤，便会诱发心脏病发作与中风。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Initially treated with skepticism, this idea now is becoming widely accepted by other scientists. Heart disease is “a disease of inflammation,” says Kathryn Moore, director of the Cardiovascular Research Center at New York University’s Grossman School of Medicine. And in the fall of 2025 the American College of Cardiology recommended that health-care providers routinely screen patients for inflammatory proteins.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">这一观点起初饱受质疑，如今已被众多科研人员广泛认可。纽约大学格罗斯曼医学院心血管研究中心主任凯瑟琳・穆尔直言，心脏病本质上是一种炎症性疾病。</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{2025}$ <font color="#548dd4">年秋季，美国心脏病学会正式建议医护人员将炎症蛋白筛查纳入常规体检项目。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">The concept brings with it renewed hope for heart therapy. If inflammation drives cardiovascular disease, then calming it might protect the organ beating in our chests. In June 2023 the U.S. Food and Drug Administration approved a new use of an inexpensive, inflammation-reducing drug for an old disease—gout—to treat patients with heart disease. In a 2020 clinical trial, researchers showed this drug, <mark style="background:rgba(136, 49, 204, 0.2)">colchicine</mark>[^3], could reduce the risk of heart attacks, strokes, and other complications by a dramatic 31 percent. And this big decrease was mostly among patients already taking the standard cholesterol-lowering medications: <mark style="background:rgba(136, 49, 204, 0.2)">statins</mark>[^4].</span>

<font color="#548dd4">这一理念为心脏疾病治疗重拾希望。若炎症是心血管疾病的诱因，那么抑制炎症或许就能守护我们胸腔中跳动的心脏。</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{2023}$ <font color="#548dd4">年</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{6}$ <font color="#548dd4">月</font>，<font color="#548dd4">美国食品药品监督管理局批准一款廉价抗炎老药（原本用于治疗痛风）新增适应症，用于医治心脏病患者。</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{2020}$ <font color="#548dd4">年一项临床试验证实，秋水仙碱可将心脏病发作、中风及其他并发症风险大幅降低</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{31\%}$<font color="#548dd4">。且这一显著疗效，大多体现在已常规服用他汀类降脂药物的患者群体中。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">But this treatment is not without controversy. Some recent studies of colchicine have not found protective effects, and many cardiologists are reluctant to use it. Ridker and other researchers are now testing different anti-inflammatory therapies with more precise modes of action. Although questions remain, many researchers believe this shift—seeing the vascular system not as a series of <mark style="background:#fdbfff">clogged</mark>[^5] pipes but as battlefields of inflammation—could transform public health and save millions of lives.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">但这种疗法并非毫无争议。近期多项关于秋水仙碱的研究并未发现其具备保护效用，众多心脏病科医生也不愿采用该药物。里德克等研究者如今正试验各类作用机制更为精准的抗炎疗法。尽管尚存诸多疑问，但不少学者认为，这种观念转变——不再将血管系统视作堵塞的管道，而是视作炎症交锋的战场——有望革新公共卫生领域，挽救数百万人的生命。</font>

<br>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">FOR MANY DECADES THE DOGMA among doctors and scientists was that atherosclerosis—the buildup of fatty substances, including cholesterol, in the arteries—was a passive, almost mechanical process, an inevitable by-product of aging. “We were taught that the <mark style="background:#fdbfff">plaque</mark>[^6] buildup in arteries was sort of like rust in a pipe,” says Jean-Claude Tardif, a cardiologist who directs the Research Center at the Montreal Heart Institute.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">数十年来，医学界与科学界一直秉持这样一种固有定论：动脉粥样硬化，即胆固醇等脂质物质在动脉血管内不断堆积，是一种被动且近乎机械的生理过程，也是衰老过程中无可避免的附带产物。蒙特利尔心脏研究所研究中心负责人、心脏病学家让-克洛德・塔尔迪夫表示：“过去我们所学的理论认为，血管内斑块堆积，就好比水管内壁滋生铁锈一般。”</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Yet there had been hints over the centuries that inflammation might play an active role in the process. In the mid1850s German pathologist Rudolf Virchow peered through his microscope at diseased blood vessels and saw angry, inflamed tissue within the plaques. In 1913 Russian pathologist Nikolai Anichkov wrote that he fed rabbits a high-cholesterol diet and found their arteries <mark style="background:#fdbfff">teeming</mark>[^7] with white blood cells, now known to be key markers of inflammation. For the most part, researchers interpreted these findings as evidence that inflammation might develop in response to atherosclerosis.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">然而数百年来，一直有迹象表明炎症在这一病变过程中起到主动作用。</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{19}$ <font color="#548dd4">世纪</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{50}$ <font color="#548dd4">年代中期，德国病理学家鲁道夫・菲尔绍借助显微镜观察病变血管，发现斑块内部存在发炎红肿的组织。</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{1913}$ <font color="#548dd4">年，俄国病理学家尼古拉・阿尼奇科夫开展实验：给兔子喂食高胆固醇食物，结果发现其动脉中遍布白细胞，如今已知白细胞是判定炎症的核心标志物。但彼时大多数学者都将这些研究结果解读为：炎症是动脉粥样硬化引发的继发性反应。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">But it was also possible that things worked the other way around. Inflammation is a complicated process. It’s the body’s built-in alarm system, activated when the immune system senses that something untoward is happening. The body recruits immune cell soldiers to the scene, which launch an attack against any unwelcome intruders and cells they’ve infected. That’s why your throat gets red and swollen when you have the flu. Sometimes this alarm system becomes overzealous—fighting too hard or too long—and ends up harming the body’s healthy tissues in the process.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">但也存在截然相反的作用机制。炎症反应十分复杂，它是人体与生俱来的警报系统，当免疫系统察觉到异常状况时便会启动。身体会调集免疫细胞奔赴患处，对外来入侵者及其侵染的细胞发起攻击，这也是患上流感后咽喉红肿发炎的原因。有时这套防御系统会反应过度，防御攻势过猛、持续过久，最终反而损伤机体自身的健康组织。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">In the mid-1990s Ridker and his colleagues showed this hyperactivity could indeed be a cause. They published a landmark paper based on data collected as part of the Physicians’ Health Study, which had been gathering health data from male doctors since 1982. The researchers looked at blood levels of C-reactive protein (CRP), a molecular flare signaling inflammation, in 543 doctors who later had a heart attack, stroke or severe blood clot in a vein. The researchers compared these measurements with the levels of CRP in 543 doctors who did not go on to have a cardiac event. Compared with men who had the lowest CRP levels, those with the highest were three times as likely as to have a heart attack and twice as likely to have a stroke.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">上世纪</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{90}$ <font color="#548dd4">年代中期，里德克及其团队证实，这种免疫过激反应确实是致病诱因。他们依托医师健康研究项目积累的数据发表了一篇具有里程碑意义的论文，该研究自</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{1982}$ <font color="#548dd4">年起持续追踪男性医师的健康数据。研究人员检测了</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{543}$ <font color="#548dd4">名后续患上心脏病、中风或严重静脉血栓的医师体内</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\ce{C}}$ <font color="#548dd4">反应蛋白浓度，该物质是提示体内炎症发作的分子标志物。再将其与另外</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{543}$ <font color="#548dd4">名未发生心血管病变的医师的指标进行对照。结果显示：相较于</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\ce{C}}$ <font color="#548dd4">反应蛋白水平最低的人群，指标最高者突发心脏病的风险高出三倍，罹患中风的风险高出两倍。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Several years later Ridker’s team studied nearly 28,000 women participating in the Women’s Health Study. The women were followed for an average of eight years. Those with the highest levels of CRP were 2.3 times more likely to have a heart attack or other serious cardiovascular problem than were women with the lowest levels.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">数年后，里德克团队又针对参与女性健康研究的近</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{28,000}$ <font color="#548dd4">女性展开调研，平均随访时长达八年。研究发现，</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{\ce{C}}$ <font color="#548dd4">反应蛋白指标最高的女性，患上心脏病及其他严重心血管疾病的风险，是指标最低人群的</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{2.3}$ <font color="#548dd4">倍。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">These findings implied that inflammation wasn’t a side effect of heart disease. It was a lethal accomplice. In 1999 University of Washington pathologist Russell Ross wrote in the <i>New England Journal of Medicine</i> that “atherosclerosis is clearly an inflammatory disease.” Since then, other studies have confirmed Ridker’s findings. “Tons of studies involving hundreds of thousands of patients invariably have shown that the higher your inflammatory markers in blood, the higher the risk of clinical events,” Tardif says.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">这些研究结果表明，炎症并非心脏病引发的附带症状，而是致命的帮凶。</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{1999}$ <font color="#548dd4">年，华盛顿大学病理学家拉塞尔・罗斯在《新英格兰医学杂志》中撰文指出：“动脉粥样硬化显然是一种炎症性疾病。” 此后诸多研究均印证了里德克的结论。塔尔迪夫表示：“数十万受试者参与的大量研究无一例外都证实，血液中炎症标志物水平越高，罹患各类临床心血管疾病的风险就越大。”</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">At that time, Ridker was also studying cholesterol-lowering statin drugs. These drugs are prescribed primarily for their ability to lower levels of LDL cholesterol, a known driver of heart disease. LDL, or low-density lipoprotein, is a molecule that carries cholesterol to tissues that need it—it helps to build cell membranes, among other crucial functions—but when there’s too much, it can <mark style="background:#fdbfff">seep</mark>[^8] into arteries and clog them.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">彼时，里德克同时也在研究降胆固醇的他汀类药物。这类药物最初获批使用，主要是因其能够降低低密度脂蛋白胆固醇水平，而该物质是诱发心脏病的明确诱因。低密度脂蛋白可将胆固醇输送至身体各处所需组织，还参与构建细胞膜等重要生理活动；但一旦含量超标，便会渗入动脉血管并造成堵塞。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Surprisingly, Ridker’s work started to suggest that statin drugs didn’t just reduce cholesterol—they damped inflammation, too. In a 2001 study, he and his colleagues showed that pravastatin, sold under the brand name Pravachol, reduced levels of CRP by 17 percent after 24 weeks. And in a 2005 study published in the <i>New England Journal of Medicine</i>, Ridker and his co-workers reported that regardless of their LDL cholesterol levels, people who took statins and had low CRP levels were less likely to have heart attacks or die from heart-related causes than people who took statins but had high CRP levels.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">出人意料的是，里德克的研究逐渐表明，他汀类药物不止能降胆固醇，还可抑制炎症。</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{2001}$ <font color="#548dd4">年一项研究显示，商用名普拉固的普伐他汀，连续服用</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{24}$ <font color="#548dd4">周可使</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\ce{C}}$ <font color="#548dd4">反应蛋白水平下降</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{17\%}$<font color="#548dd4">。</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{2005}$ <font color="#548dd4">年发表于《新英格兰医学杂志》的研究进一步证实：无论受试者低密度脂蛋白胆固醇数值高低，服用他汀且</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\ce{C}}$ <font color="#548dd4">反应蛋白偏低者，相比服药但炎症指标偏高人群，心脏病发作及心源性死亡风险更低。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">That discovery raised a radical question: Could people with healthy cholesterol levels—who typically would not be prescribed statins—still benefit from the drugs if their degree of inflammation was high?</span>

<font color="#548dd4">这一发现引出了一个颠覆性问题：对于胆固醇指标正常、原本无需服用他汀类药物的人群，若体内炎症水平偏高，服用此类药物是否依旧能获益？</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Ridker decided to find out. He and his team designed a clinical trial to see whether people with low LDL but high CRP would benefit from statin therapy. They did—and markedly so. Published in 2008 and known as JUPITER, this trial enrolled nearly 18,000 participants with low LDL and high CRP to receive either a statin or a placebo. The trial was stopped early, after following people for an average of about two years, because the statins proved so effective. Those who took them were 54  percent less likely to have a heart attack and 48 percent less likely to have a stroke. A follow-up analysis of JUPITER patients showed that people most strongly protected from heart trouble were those who had lowered both their cholesterol and their CRP levels on the drug.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">里德克决定探明答案。他带领团队开展一项临床试验，探究低密度脂蛋白偏低但</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\ce{C}}$ <font color="#548dd4">反应蛋白偏高的人群，能否从他汀类药物治疗中获益。结果证实确有显著疗效。这项</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{2008}$ <font color="#548dd4">年发表的</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\ce{JUPITER}}$ <font color="#548dd4">试验，纳入近一万八千名受试对象，受试者均为低脂蛋白、高炎症指标人群，分别服用他汀药物或安慰剂。因药效效果过于突出，试验在平均随访约两年时便提前终止：服药人群心脏病发病风险降低</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{54\%}$<font color="#548dd4">，中风风险降低</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{48\%}$<font color="#548dd4">。后续随访分析显示，服药后胆固醇与</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\ce{C}}$ <font color="#548dd4">反应蛋白指标双双下降者，心脏疾病防护效果最佳。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">At the time, many doctors <mark style="background:#fdbfff">scoffed</mark>[^9] at the notion that cholesterol-lowering drugs could do anything useful for healthy people who already had low cholesterol. “They said, ‘These people don’t have risk,’” Ridker recalls. But the statins worked—and suddenly the old map of heart disease no longer made sense.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">彼时，不少医生都对这种观点嗤之以鼻：认为降胆固醇药物，对胆固醇水平本就偏低的健康人群毫无益处。里德克回忆道：“他们直言，这类人群根本不存在患病风险。”但他汀类药物的确起效了，至此，以往对心脏病的传统认知体系瞬间不再成立。</font>

<br>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">AT FIRST GLANCE the JUPITER findings suggest that cholesterol’s true role in heart disease might be a small one. But the opposite is true: cholesterol, it turns out, is the match that lights the fire of vascular inflammation. A match in the shape of a crystal.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">乍看之下，</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{\ce{JUPITER}}$ <font color="#548dd4">试验结果似乎说明胆固醇在心脏病中起到的作用微乎其微，然而事实恰恰相反。事实证明，胆固醇正是点燃血管炎症之火的引火之物，如同晶体形态的火种。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Ordinarily cholesterol is a waxy molecular blob. But when cholesterol accumulates and becomes highly concentrated in arteries, it can take on a crystalline shape, much the way sugar crystallizes in a concentrated <mark style="background:#fdbfff">syrup</mark>[^10].</span>

<font color="#548dd4">正常情况下，胆固醇是一种蜡状分子团。可当胆固醇在动脉内不断堆积、浓度急剧升高时，便会形成结晶形态，原理和浓糖浆里析出糖晶如出一辙。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">The crystals have been difficult for laboratory scientists to see. When pathologists are preparing biological tissue to be viewed under a microscope, they typically treat their sample with alcohol as part of the process of stiffening it so it can be thinly sliced and put on a slide. But alcohol dissolves cholesterol crystals, so pathologists rarely saw them when they studied slides of human and animal blood vessels. Instead they saw empty spaces that the crystals had left behind. And at any rate, researchers believed these crystals were harmless; some even <mark style="background:#fdbfff">postulated</mark>[^11] they arose primarily after death.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">这类胆固醇结晶一直难以被实验室科研人员观测到。病理学家制备显微镜观察用生物组织样本时，通常会用酒精做硬化处理，方便切片并置于载玻片上观察。但酒精会溶解胆固醇结晶，因此他们观察人和动物血管切片时，几乎看不到结晶，只能看见结晶溶解后留下的空隙。此外，学界以往普遍认为这类结晶并无危害，部分学者甚至推测其大多是机体死亡后才形成的。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">George Abela, chief of the division of cardiology at Michigan State University, had questions about these crystals. He knew that heart attacks and strokes often happened after arterial plaques <mark style="background:#fdbfff">ruptured</mark>[^12], and he wondered whether cholesterol crystals might play a role, tearing the plaques. One day in 2004 he asked a colleague whether cholesterol might expand when it becomes crystalline, much as water expands when it forms ice. If it did, he <mark style="background:#fdbfff">surmised</mark>[^13], then cholesterol could strain and harm arterial walls.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">密歇根州立大学心脏病科主任乔治・阿贝拉一直对这类结晶心存疑惑。他深知心梗与中风常由动脉斑块破裂引发，于是猜想胆固醇结晶或许在此过程中起到作用，致使斑块破损破裂。</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{2004}$ <font color="#548dd4">年的一天，他向同事提出设想：胆固醇凝结成晶体时，是否会像水结冰一样发生体积膨胀？他推测倘若属实，膨胀的胆固醇便会牵拉、损伤动脉血管壁。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Abela recalls his colleague’s reply. “His response was, ‘No, the only [thing] on this planet that expands with crystallization is water,’ ” Abela says. That made sense to Abela. But he did go on to ask whether anyone had ever actually double-checked.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">阿贝拉回忆起同事当时的答复。他说：“对方回道：‘世上唯有水在结晶时体积会膨胀。’”这话听着合乎常理，但阿贝拉还是追问，究竟有没有人实地验证过这一说法。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">“Of course,” he says, “nobody had.” </span>

<font color="#548dd4">他说道：“可想而知，从来没人做过这项验证。”</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Abela then had to see for himself. He bought cholesterol powder (which is used for various lab experiments), melted it into liquid form, and then let the cholesterol molecules crystallize. What he discovered shocked him: When it crystallized, cholesterol took up 45 percent more space than it did as a liquid. It did indeed expand. Moreover, the crystals had sharp, needlelike edges, perfectly shaped to tear artery walls. In a groundbreaking 2009 paper, Abela and his colleagues compared the coronary arteries of patients who had died of heart disease with those of people who had died for other reasons. They found that all the patients with heart disease had blood vessels that had been perforated by cholesterol crystals. “They’re like little knives cutting their way through the surfaces of the artery,” Abela explains. “And there are thousands of them.”</span>

<font color="#548dd4">于是阿贝拉决定亲自求证。他购置实验用胆固醇粉末，将其加热熔化为液态，再静置使其分子结晶析出。实验结果令他大为震惊：胆固醇结晶后，体积比液态时增大</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{45\%}$<font color="#548dd4">，确实会发生膨胀。不仅如此，这些结晶边缘尖锐如针，极易划破动脉管壁。在</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{2009}$ <font color="#548dd4">年一篇具有开创性的论文中，他与团队对比分析了死于心脏病及其他病因患者的冠状动脉样本，发现所有心脏病患者的血管壁均被胆固醇结晶刺穿。阿贝拉解释道：“这些结晶如同无数把细小尖刀，不断划破动脉内壁，且数量成千上万。”</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Crystals do even more damage than that. A healthy human body does not make crystalline structures, so our immune systems react to them as dangerous alarm signals and target them for attack. (This sequence of events happens in gout, too—the accumulation of crystal-shaped uric acid molecules leads to pain and inflammation.) Crystals “set off an inflammatory response to alert the body that these things are accumulating—‘we need to try to contain this and clear it,’” Moore says.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">胆固醇结晶造成的危害远不止于此。健康人体本不会生成这类结晶物质，因此免疫系统会将其视作危险警报信号，随即发起免疫攻击。痛风发病机理亦是同理：尿酸分子结晶堆积，进而引发疼痛与炎症。穆尔表示，结晶会触发炎症反应，提醒身体异物正在积聚，进而启动机制进行抑制与清除。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Next, white blood cells called monocytes race to the scene and change into macrophages. These are immune cells that engulf lipids and cholesterol deposits, including the sharp-edged cholesterol. Then, Moore explains, “they call in T cells and other immune cells to join the party, basically, and that is what causes the formation of the plaque,” a messy mixture of cholesterol, fats, immune cells and dead cell debris. The macrophages also produce inflammatory chemicals, including interleukin-1 beta (IL-1β), which further strengthen the immune attack and cause the plaque to worsen.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">随后，单核白细胞迅速奔赴患处，并分化为巨噬细胞。这类免疫细胞会吞噬脂质与胆固醇沉积物，其中也包括尖锐的胆固醇结晶。穆尔解释道：“它们还会召集</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\ce{T}}$ <font color="#548dd4">细胞等其他免疫细胞一同参与反应，动脉斑块便由此形成。” 斑块是胆固醇、脂肪、免疫细胞及细胞坏死碎屑混杂而成的物质。巨噬细胞还会分泌白介素</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{-1\beta}$ <font color="#548dd4">等炎症因子，进一步加剧免疫反应，致使斑块病变持续加重。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Normally the immune system fights, wins and then retreats. But inside an artery it doesn’t know how to quit. “For reasons that we don’t really understand, this inflammatory process gets going and doesn’t get turned off,” Moore says. It “can go on for decades.” Macrophages inside the arteries start to die, releasing their crystallized cholesterol contents. The plaque becomes increasingly unstable, and eventually it can rupture. A rupture exposes blood passing by to chemicals that cause it to clot. Those blood clots can travel through the body, causing heart attacks or strokes.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">正常情况下，免疫系统发起抗争、战胜异物后便会自行消退，但在动脉血管内，免疫反应却难以终止。穆尔称：“其中缘由尚未完全探明，炎症一旦启动便无法自行平息，甚至会持续数十年之久。”血管内的巨噬细胞陆续凋亡，将体内包裹的胆固醇结晶尽数释放，致使动脉斑块稳定性持续变差，最终发生破裂。斑块破裂后释放的物质会促使流经血液凝固形成血栓，血栓随血液循环游走全身，进而诱发心梗或中风。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">These discoveries and theories of action suggest that cholesterol is not the only bad guy in the story. The immune system’s overreaction to cholesterol crystals—the chronic inflammation it causes—is potentially far more dangerous.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">这些研究发现与作用机制表明，胆固醇并非唯一致病元凶。免疫系统对胆固醇结晶产生的过度免疫反应，以及由此引发的慢性炎症，其潜在危害要大得多。</font>

<br>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">AFTER JUPITER HIGHLIGHTED the role of inflammation, Ridker wanted to see whether there was a remedy for it. Could cooling the inflammatory cycle help the heart? Because the cellular messenger IL-1β played such a key role in inflammation, he wondered whether blocking it might slow or stop the process, quieting the storm inside the arteries.</span>

$\textcolor{#548dd4}{\ce{JUPITER}}$ <font color="#548dd4">试验凸显炎症的致病作用后，里德克尔便着手探寻应对之法。抑制炎症反应能否保护心脏？鉴于细胞信使白介素</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{-1\beta}$ <font color="#548dd4">在炎症进程中占据核心地位，他设想阻断该物质或许能减缓甚至终止炎症反应，平息血管内的病变紊乱。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">A few years after JUPITER was published, Ridker and his colleagues began recruiting people for a clinical trial that would end up convincing many other experts that inflammation-targeting therapies might be the next big cardiology frontier. In the trial, known as CANTOS and published in 2017, 10,000 heart disease patients with high CRP levels — most of whom were also on statins— were split into groups. Some were regularly injected with varying dosages of canakinumab, a monoclonal antibody that reduces levels of IL-1β in the body. Others were given a placebo.</span>

$\textcolor{#548dd4}{\ce{JUPITER}}$ <font color="#548dd4">试验成果发表数年后，里德克尔及其团队开始招募受试者开展临床试验，这项试验最终让众多医学界专家确信，靶向抗炎疗法或将成为心脏病领域的前沿新方向。这项</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{2017}$ <font color="#548dd4">年公布结果的</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{\ce{CANTOS}}$ <font color="#548dd4">试验，选取一万名</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\ce{C}}$ <font color="#548dd4">反应蛋白指标偏高的心脏病患者（其中多数已在服用他汀类药物）并进行分组。部分受试者定期注射不同剂量的卡那单抗——一种可降低体内白介素</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{-1\beta}$ <font color="#548dd4">水平的单克隆抗体，其余受试者则使用安慰剂。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">After four years of the trial those who had received 150  milligrams of canakinumab were 15 percent less likely to die of heart disease or stroke compared with those who received a placebo. CANTOS “provided the first evidence in humans that blocking these inflammatory pathways would actually be beneficial,” Moore says. “It was considered a really big moment.”</span>

<font color="#548dd4">历经四年试验后，注射</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{150}$ <font color="#548dd4">毫克卡那单抗的受试者，死于心脏病与中风的概率相较安慰剂组降低</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{15\%}$<font color="#548dd4">。穆尔表示，</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{\ce{CANTOS}}$ <font color="#548dd4">试验首次在人体中证实，阻断此类炎症通路确实具备治疗益处，堪称心血管领域意义重大的里程碑成果。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Canakinumab did, however, cause side effects: those who took the drug were more likely than others to die from infections during the trial. The FDA rejected a 2018 bid to approve the use of the medication to treat heart disease.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">但卡那单抗存在副作用：试验期间，用药人群死于各类感染的风险明显更高。</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{2018}$ <font color="#548dd4">年该药申请获批用于心脏病治疗，最终遭到美国食品药品监督管理局驳回。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">But it turned out that there were other options. While CANTOS was underway, Stefan Mark Nidorf, a cardiologist affiliated with the Harry Perkins Institute of Medical Research in Perth, Australia, was pursuing a humbler approach. In a small clinical trial, he and his colleagues investigated the antiinflammatory effects of colchicine, the old gout remedy. Nidorf ’s team reasoned that because the medicine reduced inflammation, it might do the same for cardiac problems caused by cholesterol crystals. The drug, the researchers learned, did reduce the chances of coronary events in people with heart disease.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">不过后续也找到了其他治疗方案。就在</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\ce{CANTOS}}$ <font color="#548dd4">试验开展期间，供职于澳大利亚珀斯哈里・珀金斯医学研究所的心脏病学家斯特凡・马克・尼多夫，着手研究更为稳妥的治疗思路。他带领团队开展小型临床试验，探究老牌治痛风药物秋水仙碱的抗炎功效。研究团队推断，既然该药能够抑制炎症，理应也可改善胆固醇结晶诱发的心血管病症。最终研究证实，秋水仙碱确实能够降低心脏病患者发生冠脉不良事件的风险。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">For better proof, Nidorf went bigger. In 2020 he and his team published the results of a much larger trial involving 5,500 heart disease patients. Those who took low daily doses of colchicine over an average of slightly more than two years were 31 percent less likely to experience a cardiac event compared with those who took a placebo. Almost all these people were also taking statins. That was important because it showed that colchicine could lower the chance of heart attacks and strokes among people who were already reducing their risk through cholesterol-lowering drugs.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">为获取更确凿的证据，尼多夫开展了规模更大的试验。</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{2020}$ <font color="#548dd4">年，他的团队公布了一项涵盖</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{5500}$ <font color="#548dd4">名心脏病患者的大型临床试验结果。受试者日均服用小剂量秋水仙碱，平均随访两年有余，相较于安慰剂组，其心血管不良事件发生率降低</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{31\%}$<font color="#548dd4">。这些受试者几乎均在服用他汀类降脂药物。这一结果意义重大，证实即便患者已借助降脂药管控患病风险，联用秋水仙碱仍能进一步减少心梗与中风发作。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">“Atherosclerosis is a two-sided coin,” Nidorf says. “You’ve got to lower cholesterol because that drives the inflammatory process. But doing that alone isn’t enough—you’ve really got to add in the anti-inflammatory drug.” The fact that most people in the trial were already on statins suggested that colchicine had effects far above and beyond what statins could achieve.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">尼多夫表示：“动脉粥样硬化犹如一枚双面硬币。一方面必须降低胆固醇，因其是诱发炎症的根源；但仅做到这点远远不够，还需搭配抗炎药物协同治疗。” 试验中绝大多数患者本就长期服用他汀类药物，这也印证了秋水仙碱的疗效远超他汀类药物本身所能达到的效果。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">In 2023, based in part on these findings, the FDA approved the use of colchicine to reduce the risk of heart attack, stroke, coronary revascularization, and cardiovascular death in patients with atherosclerotic disease or multiple risk factors for heart disease. That was followed two years later by the American College of Cardiology’s recommendation that health-care providers routinely screen patients’ CRP levels—a striking sign of how far the field has come.</span>

$\textcolor{#548dd4}{5500}$ <font color="#548dd4">年，依托上述多项研究成果，美国食品药品监督管理局正式批准秋水仙碱用于降低动脉粥样硬化患者及多项心脏病高危人群的心梗、中风、冠脉血运重建手术风险与心血管死亡风险。时隔两年，美国心脏病学会进一步建议医护人员常规筛查患者</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\ce{C}}$ <font color="#548dd4">反应蛋白水平，这也标志着心血管疾病诊疗领域已迎来重大转变。</font>

<br>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">BUT SCIENCE RARELY moves in straight lines. Since then, additional clinical trials involving colchicine have been published, and results have been mixed. In a clinical trial known as CLEAR SYNERGY, which was published in 2025 and involved 7,000 patients who had recently had a heart attack, researchers found that the use of colchicine for an average of three years did not reduce the participants’ risk of a coronary event.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">但科学的发展很少一帆风顺。此后，更多关于秋水仙碱的临床试验陆续发表，结果却喜忧参半。</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{2025}$ <font color="#548dd4">年公布的</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\ce{CLEAR SYNERGY}}$ <font color="#548dd4">试验纳入</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{7000}$ <font color="#548dd4">名近期发作心梗的患者，研究发现：平均服用秋水仙碱三年，并未降低受试者冠脉不良事件的发生风险。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">“We were surprised, given the prior studies,” says the trial’s principal investigator, Sanjit Jolly, an interventional cardiologist at McMaster University in Ontario. The trial also raised concerns about colchicine’s gastrointestinal side effects. “Between 10 and 15 percent of patients get quite severe, quite significant diarrhea,” he adds. (Colchicine reduces inflammation by disrupting the formation of cellular structures called microtubules in immune cells. Microtubules are also essential to rapidly dividing cells that line the intestines, however, so people taking the drug may have GI problems.)</span>

<font color="#548dd4">该试验首席研究员、安大略省麦克马斯特大学介入心脏病学家桑吉特・乔利表示：“结合以往研究来看，这个结果着实出乎我们意料。”此项试验还暴露出秋水仙碱存在的胃肠道副作用隐患。他补充道：“有一成至一成五的患者会出现严重且明显的腹泻症状。”秋水仙碱通过破坏免疫细胞内微管结构来抑制炎症，但肠道内壁增殖旺盛的细胞同样离不开微管，因此服药人群极易出现肠胃不适。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Therein lies a frequent difficulty with anti-inflammatory drugs. “When you’re affecting inflammation, you’re affecting the immune system,” says Gregg Stone, an interventional cardiologist at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai. It’s often challenging to find a therapy “that hits the sweet spot of being effective without being too toxic.”</span>

<font color="#548dd4">这正是抗炎药物普遍存在的难题。西奈山伊坎医学院介入心脏病学家格雷格・斯通表示：“干预炎症反应，本质就是干预免疫系统。” 想要找到疗效理想且毒性温和、恰到好处的治疗方案，向来绝非易事。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Perhaps because of the potential side effects, recent data suggest that cardiologists rarely prescribe colchicine. “I would say the general cardiology community has a lot of doubts right now,” Jolly says.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">或许正是由于存在这些潜在副作用，近期数据显示，心脏病学家很少开具秋水仙碱处方。乔利说：“我得说，目前整个心脏病学界对此疑虑重重。”</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">The CLEAR SYNERGY trial has exposed fault lines in the field. Ridker, Nidorf, and others argue that the trial had serious limitations. A major one was that it was conducted during the COVID-19 pandemic, when people might have been less likely to report heart-related issues to their doctors. It also focused on acute inflammation just after heart attacks, not the chronic inflammation of atherosclerosis. Colchicine may simply not offer benefits in that context, Nidorf says.</span>

$\textcolor{#548dd4}{\ce{CLEAR SYNERGY}}$ <font color="#548dd4">试验暴露出该研究领域存在的分歧裂痕。里德克尔、尼多夫等学者认为这项试验存在明显缺陷，一大关键问题是试验开展恰逢新冠疫情期间，患者大概率疏于向医生报备心脏相关不适。此外，该试验仅聚焦心梗发作后的急性炎症，并未针对动脉粥样硬化引发的慢性炎症展开研究。尼多夫表示，在这类情形下，秋水仙碱本就难以显现疗效。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">But other researchers say CLEAR SYNERGY produced sound conclusions, and it means that more research on colchicine is needed. “If you do have very high CRP, it’s probably a reasonable drug to try, but I just wouldn’t use it in everybody,” Stone says.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">但另有研究者认为，</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{\ce{CLEAR SYNERGY}}$ <font color="#548dd4">试验结论确凿，这也意味着秋水仙碱仍需开展更多深入研究。斯通称：“若患者</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\ce{C}}$ <font color="#548dd4">反应蛋白指标显著偏高，选用该药尚且合理，但绝不适用于所有人群。”</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">There are leads about different therapies. Several multinational clinical trials are underway to test the effects of inhibitors of interleukin-6, another of the immune system’s chemical messengers, on heart disease outcomes. IL-6 signaling drives a number of harmful vascular processes, and it’s an important link between IL-1β and CRP activity. It could be more closely tied to cardiovascular risks, so researchers hope IL-6 blockers will be more effective than other drugs. Some trials are being funded and overseen by large pharmaceutical companies, including Novo Nordisk and Novartis, but no results have been published yet.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">目前已有新型疗法研究线索。多项跨国临床试验正在推进，用以测试白介素</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{-6}$ <font color="#548dd4">抑制剂这类免疫信号物质抑制剂对心血管疾病预后的作用。白介素</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{-6}$ <font color="#548dd4">信号通路会诱发多种有害血管病变，也是连接白介素</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{-1\beta}$ <font color="#548dd4">与</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\ce{C}}$ <font color="#548dd4">反应蛋白作用的关键枢纽，和心血管发病风险关联更为紧密。因此研究人员期望白介素</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{-6}$ <font color="#548dd4">阻断剂疗效能优于现有药物。部分试验由诺和诺德、诺华等大型药企出资并统筹监管，不过相关研究结果尚未正式发布。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Ultimately the hope is not merely to prevent heart attacks and strokes but to stop atherosclerosis and heart disease before they start. To that end, Tardif recently started a clinical trial involving 10,000 patients with type 2 diabetes to see whether colchicine can prevent heart disease in such people. Diabetes is a prime risk factor for cardiac trouble. “We’re trying to show that reducing inflammation very early in the process is going to help,” he says. At N.Y.U., Moore’s lab is trying to answer a related question: Is it possible to find drugs that can not only help to stop the immune fire but also repair the damage it’s already done?</span>

<font color="#548dd4">归根结底，人们的愿景不只是预防心梗与中风，更是从根源上阻断动脉粥样硬化与心脏病的发生。为此，塔尔迪夫近期启动一项临床试验，纳入一万名</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{2}$ <font color="#548dd4">型糖尿病患者，探究秋水仙碱能否为这类人群起到心脏病预防作用。糖尿病是诱发心脏疾病的首要高危因素。他表示：“我们旨在证实，在病变早期及时抗炎干预能够起到防护作用。”纽约大学穆尔的实验室也在钻研相关课题：能否研发出一类药物，既能平息体内免疫炎症反应，又能修复炎症已然造成的机体损伤？</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Ridker thinks much of that is possible. After decades of skepticism, he is enjoying seeing his once radical idea go mainstream. The change “is very exciting,” he says. “Twenty years ago people thought we were just nuts.”</span>

<font color="#548dd4">里德克尔认为这些设想大多有望实现。历经数十年质疑，他欣喜地见证自己昔日激进的学术观点如今已然成为主流。他坦言：“这一转变着实令人振奋，二十年前，所有人都觉得我们异想天开。”</font>
