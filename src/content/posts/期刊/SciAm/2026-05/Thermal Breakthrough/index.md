<p style="margin:0!important;text-indent:0!important;font-family: 'Gulliver' !important;"><span style="background:#000000;color:#ffffff;font-weight:bold;padding:0 4px;user-select:none;">CHEMISTRY</span></p>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">**COPPER MOVES THE WORLD' S HEAT**. For more than a century it has been recognized as one of the best heat conductors in nature, and this property made copper the go-to choice to cool electronics, industrial equipment, data centers and power systems. But there’s a new, record-breakingly cool metal in town.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">铜，输送着全球的热量。一个多世纪以来，铜一直被公认为自然界导热性能最优的材料之一。凭借这一特性，它始终是电子设备、工业器械、数据中心与电力系统散热的首选。但如今，一种导热性能创下新纪录的新型金属已然出现。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">As reported in *Science*, a metallic material called θ-phase tantalum nitride achieved a thermal conductivity of about 1,110 watts per meter-kelvin—about three times higher than copper’s 400 watts per meter-kelvin. And it works in a way scientists have never seen before. “Our result breaks the historic ceiling for heat transport in metallic materials,” says senior author Yongjie Hu, a physicist and engineer at the University of California, Los Angeles. “Given [this conductor’s] superior performance, it has the potential to complement or even replace copper.”</span>

<font color="#548dd4">据《科学》期刊报道，一种 θ 相氮化钽金属材料，热导率达到约 1110 W/(m・K)，约为铜（400 W /(m・K)）的三倍。而且它的导热机制是科学家前所未见的。加州大学洛杉矶分校物理学家兼工程学家、论文通讯作者胡永杰表示：“我们的成果打破了金属材料热传导的历史上限。凭借这款导热材料的优异性能，它有望补充甚至替代铜。”</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Similar to the way pure carbon can form diamonds, graphene, or other structures, tantalum nitride exists in multiple forms with very different properties. Scientists have known about some of those forms for decades, but until now no one had studied the specific configuration Hu and his colleagues focused on, in which the material’s atoms are arranged in a continuous, highly ordered crystal lattice. In this structure, the researchers found that both electrons and packets of vibrational energy called <mark style="background:rgba(136, 49, 204, 0.2)">phonons</mark>[^1], which carry heat and sound, encountered less resistance compared with conventional metals, letting them conduct heat better.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">就像纯碳能形成钻石、石墨烯或其他结构一样，氮化钽也存在多种物相形态，彼此性能差异巨大。其中部分形态早已为科学界所知，但直到现在，还没人研究过胡永杰团队所聚焦的这种特殊原子构型：材料原子排布呈连续且高度有序的晶体晶格。研究人员发现，在这种结构中，电子以及传递热量与声波的声子（振动能量包），相比传统金属受到的阻碍更小，因此导热效率大幅提升。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">The material also revealed a previously unexplored strategy to boost to metallic heat conduction. In ordinary metals, phonons are frequently disrupted by collisions with one another and with electrons, reducing their ability to dissipate heat. In the form of tantalum nitride that Hu and his colleagues studied, the atomic structure of the crystal lattice lets phonons travel unusually long distances with minimal interference. This discovery provides a new direction for scientists designing next-generation thermal materials, Hu says.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">这种材料还揭示了一种此前从未被探索过的提升金属导热能力的全新机理。在普通金属中，声子之间会频繁相互碰撞，还会与电子发生碰撞，从而削弱其散热能力。而在胡永杰团队研究的这种氮化钽物相中，特殊的晶体晶格原子结构，能让声子以极低干扰、传播超乎寻常的超长距离。胡永杰表示，这一发现为科研人员研发新一代导热材料指明了全新方向。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">The study’s findings “are both exceptional and conceptually important,” says Xiaojia Wang, a mechanical engineer at the University of Minnesota who was not involved in the research. The team rigorously verified its measurements, which suggests they are robust, she says, and if the material can be manufactured at scale, it could have a “substantial impact” on thermal management for electronics, data centers and energy systems.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">明尼苏达大学机械工程师王晓佳并未参与此项研究，她评价称，该研究成果既极具突破性，又具备重要理论价值。她表示，研究团队对测量结果进行了严谨验证，数据结论可靠扎实；若这种材料能够实现规模化制备，将对电子设备、数据中心及能源系统的热管理领域产生重大影响。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Hu adds that θ-phase tantalum nitride could be especially valuable as artificial intelligence gains even more widespread use and heat dissipation becomes a data-center bottleneck. For materials scientists, the work could also provide inspiration to challenge other long-standing constraints. “Do we truly understand where the real limits lie, or do the boundaries assumed for decades to be fundamental simply reflect our current tools and understanding?” Hu asks. “Now that the record has been broken for heat conduction, it makes us rethink whether other assumed boundaries in materials physics could also be broken.”</span>

<font color="#548dd4">胡永杰补充道，随着人工智能应用愈发普及，散热逐渐成为数据中心的性能瓶颈，θ 相氮化钽的价值将尤为突出。对于材料科学家而言，这项研究也为突破其他长期存在的固有限制提供了启发。他提出：“我们是真的摸清了材料性能的极限所在，还是说，那些数十年来被视作底层规律的边界，其实只是受限于我们当下的研究手段与认知水平？”“如今导热纪录已被打破，这也让我们开始重新思考，材料物理中其他那些公认的既定边界，是否同样有被突破的可能。”</font>

<p style="font-family: 'Gulliver' !important;" align="right">—<i>Rachel Nuwer</i></p>

[^1]: <span style="font-family: Gulliver;">**phonon**</span>：声子，晶体晶格振动的量子化能量单元
