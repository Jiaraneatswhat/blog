<p style="margin:0!important;text-indent:0!important;font-family: 'Gulliver' !important;"><span style="background:#000000;color:#ffffff;font-weight:bold;padding:0 4px;user-select:none;">BIOLOGY</span></p>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">**WHEN YOU GET UP** out of a chair, your blood pressure instantly increases to compensate for the movement and keep you from fainting. For decades researchers thought neurons called <mark style="background:rgba(136, 49, 204, 0.2)">baroreceptors</mark>[^1] in arteries controlled this process by signaling the sudden drop in blood pressure to the brain, which then issues orders to constrict blood vessels. Some experts suspected that other neurons might help maintain blood pressure during such shifts in posture, but they didn’t know which ones.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">当你从椅子上站起身时，血压会瞬间升高，以此补偿身体姿态的变化，避免眩晕昏倒。数十年来，研究人员一直认为，动脉中一种名为压力感受器神经元的细胞主导这一过程：它向大脑传递血压骤降的信号，随后大脑下达指令，让血管收缩。也有部分专家猜测，体态发生这类变化时，可能还有其他神经元参与维持血压平衡，但此前一直无法确定具体是哪一种。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">For a study published recently in *Nature*, scientists took a close look at mysterious neurons in the heart that could be involved. “They have been known to exist, but we had no idea what they were sensing,” says study senior author Stephen Liberles, a molecular neuroscientist at Harvard University. Working with mice, his team focused on a protein called PIEZO2 that converts pressure on cell membranes into nerve signals. The researchers found PIEZO2-expressing neurons wrapping around all four chambers of the mouse heart in intricate, weblike structures. “There are such neurons in the human heart as well,” Liberles says.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">近期发表在《自然》期刊上的一项研究中，科学家对心脏内一类作用未知的神秘神经元展开了深入研究。该研究资深作者、哈佛大学分子神经科学家斯蒂芬・利伯尔斯表示：“我们早就知道这类神经元存在，却一直不清楚它们能感知什么信号。”他的团队以小鼠为实验对象，重点研究一种名为 PIEZO2 的蛋白质，这种蛋白能将细胞膜受到的压力转化为神经信号。研究人员发现，表达 PIEZO2 的神经元以错综复杂的网状结构，缠绕包裹着小鼠心脏的四个腔室。利伯尔斯称，人类心脏中同样存在这类神经元。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">To understand the neurons’ function, Liberles and his colleagues rotated mice from horizontal to upright while monitoring their vitals in real time. A healthy mouse instantly adjusts its heart rate to compensate for such a posture change. When the researchers injected mice with a toxin that selectively killed the PIEZO2 neurons in the heart, however, their blood pressure <mark style="background:#fdbfff">plummeted</mark>[^2], and they failed to recover. When the researchers induced <mark style="background:#fdbfff">hemorrhaging</mark>[^3] in the mice, the PIEZO2 neurons acted long before sensors in the arteries would have been able to sound the alarm—suggesting that they have a direct line on blood volume.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">为探明这类神经元的功能，利伯尔斯及其团队将小鼠从平躺姿态旋转至直立姿态，同时实时监测它们的生命体征。健康的小鼠会立刻调整心率，以代偿这种体位变化。然而，当研究人员向小鼠注射一种毒素、特异性杀死心脏中的 PIEZO2 神经元后，小鼠血压便骤然暴跌，且无法自行恢复。研究人员还对小鼠进行了诱发出血实验，结果发现：PIEZO2 神经元的反应远早于动脉压力感受器发出预警 —— 这说明这类神经元能直接感知血容量变化。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">The team suspects that the brain most likely gets a general sense of blood pressure from the arteries but relies on these newly discovered heart sensors for high-<mark style="background:#fdbfff">fidelity</mark>[^4] updates on the volume of blood moving through the organ.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">研究团队推测，大脑大概率从动脉获取整体血压概况，但依靠这些新发现的心脏感受器，高精准地实时更新流经心脏的血容量变化。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">“The authors conclude that PIEZO2-expressing neurons are required to maintain blood pressure and support survival during blood-volume depletion,” says Ardem Patapoutian, a molecular biologist at the Scripps Research Institute, who was not involved in the study. Patapoutian won the 2021 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for discovering the role PIEZO1 and PIEZO2 proteins play in sensing mechanical stimuli. “This is an important study that offers valuable insights into cardiovascular regulation,” he adds. But our hunt for mysterious circulatory neurons is far from over.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">斯克里普斯研究所的分子生物学家阿德姆・帕塔普蒂安并未参与这项研究，他表示：“研究作者得出结论，在血容量减少时，表达 PIEZO2 的神经元是维持血压、维系生存所必需的。”帕塔普蒂安因发现 PIEZO1 与 PIEZO2 蛋白在感知机械刺激中的作用，荣获 2021 年诺贝尔生理学或医学奖。他补充道：“这是一项意义重大的研究，为心血管调节机制提供了极具价值的见解。”但人类对循环系统中神秘神经元的探索，远远没有结束。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Liberles and his colleagues note in their study that there are at least six different neuron types in the cardiovascular system, and we still don’t know the function of three of those six. “We want to know how they work,” Liberles says. “There’s a lot to do.”</span>

<font color="#548dd4">利伯尔斯及其团队在研究中指出，心血管系统内至少存在六种不同类型的神经元，其中三种的功能目前仍不为人知。利伯尔斯表示：“我们想弄清楚它们的运作机制，前路还有大量研究待完成。”</font>

[^2]: <span style="font-family: Gulliver;">**plummet** /ˈplʌmɪt/: v. to fall or drop suddenly and steeply in amount, value or level</span>

[^3]: <span style="font-family: Gulliver;">**hemorrhage** /ˈhemərɪdʒ/: v. bleeding, especially a large or uncontrolled loss of blood from the body</span>

[^4]: <span style="font-family: Gulliver;">**fidelity** /fɪˈdeləti/: n. the quality of being accurate and exact</span>
