<p style="margin:0!important;text-indent:0!important;font-family: 'Gulliver' !important;"><span style="background:#000000;color:#ffffff;font-weight:bold;padding:0 4px;user-select:none;">MATERIALS SCIENCE</span></p>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">**“COMPLEXIMERS”—MATERIALS** that can be molded like window glass but that resist impacts like plastic does— shouldn’t exist, researchers say. Nevertheless, a few grams of one such substance sit in a laboratory at Wageningen University in the Netherlands.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">研究人员表示，“复合聚合物材料” —— 一种既能像窗玻璃一样塑形，又能像塑料一样抗冲击的材料，本不该在自然界中存在。但荷兰瓦赫宁根大学的实验室里，却已经制备出了数克这种新型物质。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">In *Nature Communications*, Wageningen physical chemist Jasper van der Gucht and his team describe what makes compleximers as meltable as glass yet as hard to break as plastic. Someday this paradoxical stuff could make it easier to fashion and fix sturdy protective gear such as helmets.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">瓦赫宁根大学物理化学家贾斯珀・范德古赫特及其团队在《自然・通讯》期刊中，阐释了复合聚合物为何既能像玻璃一样熔融塑形，又能像塑料般坚韧抗裂。未来这种自相矛盾特性的新材料，有望让头盔等坚固防护装备的成型制造与维修变得更加简便。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Window glass, called silica, and most plastics are “glassy” materials— when they cool from their liquid states, they don’t solidify into crystals with neatly arranged atoms like water does when it freezes into ice. Instead they form an <mark style="background:#fdbfff">amorphous</mark>[^1] mass that feels like a solid but has randomly arranged atoms like a liquid.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">窗玻璃的主要成分是二氧化硅，它和大多数塑料都属于玻璃态材料：这类物质从液态冷却时，不会像水结冰那样，凝固成原子排列规整的晶体。相反，它们会形成非晶态物质，外观和质感如同固体，内部原子排布却像液体一样杂乱无章。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">For decades scientists have thought, based on experimental observations, that the lower a glassy material’s melting rate, the less impact it can bear. Both slow-melting window glass and faster- melting plastic abide by this rule: the former changes state slowly but shatters easily, whereas the latter solidifies and melts abruptly but can better withstand impact. But van der Gucht and his team found that compleximers completely defy this law. The trick could lie in the material’s structure: its long chains of molecules, called polymers, are held together by a farreaching kind of bond.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">数十年来，科学家依据实验观测一直认为：玻璃态材料的熔融速率越慢，抗冲击能力就越弱。熔融缓慢的窗玻璃、熔融较快的塑料，都遵循这一规律：前者物态变化缓慢，但易碎崩裂；后者凝固和熔融过程突变剧烈，却更能承受冲击。但范德古赫特团队发现，复合聚合物完全打破了这一规律。其奥秘或许在于材料自身结构：它长长的分子链（即聚合物），依靠一种作用范围极远的键合方式联结在一起。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">The researchers initially created compleximers, a term they coined, as an easily recyclable alternative to a type of plastic called <mark style="background:#fdbfff">thermoset</mark>[^2]. Thermosets are made up of polymer chains held tightly together by extremely hard-to-break chemical bonds, which makes them very stable but hard to recycle. The researchers added charged molecules, which made the chains cling to one another using an ionic, “opposites attract” type of bond instead, and they incorporated water-repelling compounds to stop the chains from disintegrating in water. The charged molecules’ionic interactions— which hold over longer distances than the previous chemical bonds—may help compleximers stay compact rather than rapidly expanding to melt immediately when heated, the team suggests.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">研究人员将这类新材料命名为复合聚体，这一名称由他们首创。研发初衷是为热固性塑料寻找一种易于回收的替代材料。热固性塑料的聚合物分子链，由极难断裂的化学键紧密结合，这让它性质十分稳定，却难以回收再利用。研究人员在材料中加入带电分子，使分子链依靠异性相吸的离子键相互结合；同时添加疏水化合物，避免分子链在水中分解溃散。团队认为：带电分子间的离子相互作用，作用距离远大于传统化学键；这一特性让复合聚体结构保持紧实，受热时不会快速膨胀、瞬间熔化。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Ionic interactions could improve the mechanical properties of glass-forming materials and make them easier to work with, says University of Chicago chemical engineer Matthew Tirrell, who was not involved in the work.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">芝加哥大学化学工程师马修・蒂雷尔（并未参与此项研究）表示：离子相互作用有望提升玻璃态材料的力学性能，同时让这类材料更易于加工成型。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">The slow melting also means compleximer-based objects are easier to fix than ones made of thermosets; “just by heating it with a heat gun, you can repair a scratch or a crack,” van der Gucht says.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">这种缓慢熔融的特性也意味着，由复合聚体制成的物品比热固性塑料制品更易修复。范德古赫特称：“只需用热风枪加热，就能修复划痕或裂缝。”</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Both researchers say this rule-defying material could also give physicists a better understanding of how glass forms, a phenomenon called the <mark style="background:rgba(136, 49, 204, 0.2)">glass transition</mark>[^3]. Finding these long-range interactions that make glassy materials melt differently, van der Gucht says, “should help theorists explain the glass transition in a more general sense.”</span>

<font color="#548dd4">两位研究人员均表示，这种打破固有规律的新材料，也能让物理学家更深入地理解玻璃化转变这一现象，探究玻璃的形成机制。范德古赫特称，这类能改变玻璃态材料熔融特性的长程相互作用，“将助力理论学家从更普适的角度阐释玻璃化转变的原理”。</font>
