<strong>A newly proposed class of molecules could shake up cell biology</strong>
</span>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">IN SCIENCE, WHETHER an anomaly is insignificant or the basis for a promising new field of study can <mark style="background:rgba(136, 49, 204, 0.2)">boil down to</mark>[^1] the catchiness of its name. Pick the wrong one, and conferences are hard to organise and funding <mark style="background:#fdbfff">shrivels</mark>[^2] up. But pick the right one, and the publicity takes care of itself.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">在科学界，一种异常现象究竟是无足轻重，还是有望开辟全新研究领域，往往取决于其命名是否吸睛。名字取得差劲，不仅难以举办学术会议，科研经费也会日渐枯竭；倘若命名巧妙，热度与关注度自然随之而来。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">In that spirit, say hello to peptideins: a newly named class of molecules found within human cells that are similar to proteins but smaller and with vaguer purposes. As the authors of the paper that named them, published in the journal *Nature* this week, point out, they may still be important. Sebastiaan van Heesch, a protein specialist at the Princess Máxima Centre in the Netherlands who co-led the new study, has said that peptideins might “unlock new insights and drug targets across human biology”, potentially assisting with the development of immunotherapies and vaccines against cancer. What is certain for now is that they complicate the conventional picture of how cells work.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">秉持这一思路，来认识肽质：这是一类新近定名、存在于人体细胞内的分子，结构近似蛋白质，但体型更小，功能作用也尚不明确。本周刊发于《自然》期刊的相关定名论文指出，这类分子依旧具备重要研究价值。荷兰玛克西玛公主中心的蛋白质领域专家、该研究联合负责人塞巴斯蒂安・范赫施表示，肽素分子有望助力人类生物学领域挖掘全新研究思路与药物作用靶点，或能推动癌症免疫疗法与抗癌疫苗的研发进程。目前可以确定的是，它们打破了人们对细胞运作机制的传统认知。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Simply put, enzymes within a cell are thought to copy strands of DNA into molecules of RNA. These in turn are used as blueprints to make chains of amino acids known as peptides. (This second process, known as translation, is largely handled by cellular components called ribosomes.) Proteins are a loosely defined subset of peptides: those that are of a certain size; have a known function; and can be found across several species.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">简单来说，细胞内的酶会将</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\text{DNA}}$ <font color="#548dd4">链转录为</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\text{RNA}}$ <font color="#548dd4">分子，</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{\text{RNA}}$ <font color="#548dd4">再作为模板合成氨基酸链，也就是肽链。这第二步过程即翻译，主要由细胞内的核糖体完成。蛋白质属于肽类里界定较宽泛的一类：需达到特定长度、具备明确功能，且在多个物种中普遍存在。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">There are about 19,500 recognised human proteins, each of which shares its name with its functional gene—the stretch of DNA responsible for making it. Many are important. The p53 protein responds to DNA damage and either pauses cell growth and division or triggers cell death as a way to suppress cancer. Insulin, a hormone, is a protein that regulates blood sugar by instructing cells to absorb glucose.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">目前已确认的人类蛋白质约有</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{19500}$ <font color="#548dd4">种，每种蛋白都与其功能基因同名，这类基因即是负责合成对应蛋白质的</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\text{DNA}}$ <font color="#548dd4">片段。其中诸多蛋白作用关键。</font>$\textcolor{#548dd4}{\text{p}53}$ <font color="#548dd4">蛋白可感知</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\text{DNA}}$ <font color="#548dd4">损伤，或暂停细胞增殖分裂，或启动细胞凋亡，以此抑制癌变；激素胰岛素同样属于蛋白质，它能指令细胞摄取葡萄糖，从而调节血糖水平。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">For years cell biologists focused on the bits of the genome that were known to code for the proteins, with the rest dismissed as junk. But better experimental tools have revealed cracks in this simple picture and shown that DNA has valuable functions beyond protein manufacture.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">多年来，细胞生物学家只专注于基因组中已知编码蛋白质的片段，其余序列都被视作无用的垃圾</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\text{DNA}}$<font color="#548dd4">。而如今更先进的实验手段推翻了这一简易认知，证实</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\text{DNA}}$ <font color="#548dd4">除合成蛋白质外，还具备诸多重要功能。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">A technique known as ribosome profiling, for example, which can reveal the precise spot on an RNA strand where translation is under way, allowed researchers to map exactly where the cell’s protein-making machinery is active. It revealed that translation might be happening in genome regions far from known genes. At the same time, increasingly sensitive mass spectrometry experiments allowed for ever smaller molecules to be spotted in cell samples. Together, these techniques shifted the focus towards sections of DNA capable of making molecules that look like miniature proteins.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">例如核糖体图谱分析技术，能够精准定位</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\text{RNA}}$ <font color="#548dd4">链上正在进行翻译的位点，助力研究者精确定位细胞蛋白合成机制的活跃区域。该技术发现，翻译活动可发生在远离已知基因的基因组区段。与此同时，精度不断提升的质谱分析，也让科研人员能在细胞样本中检出体量愈发微小的分子。两类技术结合，让研究重心转向了可合成类微型蛋白分子的</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\text{DNA}}$ <font color="#548dd4">片段。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">The dark proteome, as this collection of “microproteins” is known, has <mark style="background:#fdbfff">befuddled</mark>[^3] scientists even as it has grown. Though they were once dismissed as unworthy of attention, recent studies have suggested that microproteins could be concealing drivers of disease. They could be fruitful targets for future drugs to aim at.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">这类微型蛋白统称暗蛋白质组，其数量不断增多，也一直令科学家倍感困惑。它们从前被视作无关紧要、无人深究，而近期研究表明，微型蛋白或许暗藏致病关键因子，也有望成为未来药物研发极具潜力的作用靶点。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Dr van Heesch and his colleagues have produced the most detailed map of this dark proteome thus far. By pooling and analysing the results of previous experiments they confirmed the existence of 1,785 microproteins. Their paper reveals just how small some of these are: about 65 per cent of known microproteins held fewer than 50 amino acids. That is smaller than over 99 per cent of the 19,500 known proteins.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">范赫施博士及其团队绘制出了迄今为止最详尽的暗蛋白质组图谱。他们整合分析过往实验数据，证实了</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{1785}$ <font color="#548dd4">种微型蛋白真实存在。该论文还揭示了这类分子的体型极小：已知微型蛋白中约</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{65\%}$ <font color="#548dd4">的氨基酸数量不足</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{50}$ <font color="#548dd4">个，远少于</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{19500}$ <font color="#548dd4">种已知常规蛋白质里</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{99\%}$ <font color="#548dd4">的蛋白长度。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Some well-studied microproteins are already known to be biologically useful. One, called ASNSD1-uORF, is involved in the progression of the childhood brain cancer medulloblastoma. Another, humanin, protects cells from stress and may play a role in healthy ageing as well as the onset of neurodegenerative diseases.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">部分研究较为透彻的微型蛋白，现已证实具备生理功能。其中名为</font> $\textcolor{#548dd4}{\text{ASNSD1-uORF}}$ <font color="#548dd4">的微型蛋白，与儿童脑部肿瘤髓母细胞瘤的病情进展相关；还有护脑素，可保护细胞抵御应激损伤，既有助于机体健康衰老，也和神经退行性疾病的发病息息相关。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">The vast majority of discovered microproteins, however, have not been linked to any concrete biological effects. It is this group that Dr van Heesch and his colleagues have dubbed the peptideins. By formalising them in this way, the researchers want to encourage the researchers who compile protein databases to study them as well as to take their possible roles in health and disease more seriously.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">然而绝大多数已发现的微型蛋白，尚未明确具备具体生理作用。范赫施团队便将这类分子正式命名为肽质。研究者此番定名归类，意在推动蛋白数据库整理人员重视这类物质，同时促使学界深入探究其在健康与疾病中潜在的生理作用。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Many of the peptideins confirmed so far suggest this approach may have value. Some are expressed and displayed on the outside of tumour cells, for example, which could allow them to be recognised by the body’s immune system. These could offer new targets to help immunotherapy drugs locate and remove such cancerous cells. It is also possible, the researchers suggest, that peptideins might regulate the activity of other genes or influence cell signalling.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">目前已确认的诸多肽素，印证了这一研究方向极具价值。例如部分肽素会在肿瘤细胞表面表达显现，能被人体免疫系统识别，有望成为全新靶点，助力免疫治疗药物精准定位并清除癌细胞。研究人员还提出，肽素或可调控其他基因活性，亦能参与调控细胞信号传导。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">Just how many peptideins there are remains an open question. Some experts warn that ribosome profiling is prone to flagging false positives—what looks like translation occurring at a surprising site, in other words, may be little more than an illusion. Indeed, this week’s paper identifies thousands of possible peptidein sightings that could not be confirmed, though future search methods may be better equipped to do so. Many peptideins may also turn out to be <mark style="background:rgba(136, 49, 204, 0.2)">damp squibs</mark>[^4]. For David Tollervey, a cell biologist at the University of Edinburgh, it is unlikely that more than a few hundred prove useful.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">肽素的实际总数目前尚无定论。部分专家提醒，核糖体图谱分析容易出现假阳性结果，也就是说，那些看似发生在特殊位点的翻译活动，或许只是观测假象。此次发表的论文确实筛查出数千个疑似肽素位点，但均未能证实，不过未来的检测技术或将完成验证。不少肽素最终也可能被证实并无实际作用。爱丁堡大学细胞生物学家戴维・托勒维便认为，最终真正具备研究价值的肽素，大概率仅有数百种。</font>

<span style="font-family: Gulliver;">A better understanding of peptideins will determine whether or not the name catches on. If they prove to be largely unimportant, it may soon be forgotten. But if studying them pays off, prepare to hear the name a lot more.</span>

<font color="#548dd4">对肽素的研究深入程度，将决定这一名称能否广为流传。倘若最终证实其大多无实际价值，这个名词很快便会被人淡忘；可一旦相关研究取得重大成果，日后我们便会频繁听到这个叫法。</font>
